## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Dig, Dig, Die**. A gameplay trainer for treasure raids, offering configurable survival, movement, exploration, and resource options while preserving the game's co-op digging and extraction loop.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, easier cooperative treasure runs, faster progression, or additional control over the game's horror and survival difficulty.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **God Mode** — Prevents the controlled player from taking damage during graveyard raids.
- **Unlimited Treasure** — Keeps collected treasure available for post-raid spending and equipment progression.
- **Fast Movement** — Increases player movement speed for quicker traversal through graveyards.
- **Silent Movement** — Reduces or suppresses the player's sound activity, helping avoid monsters that react to noise.
- **Freeze Raid Timer** — Pauses the active raid timer when supported by the trainer version.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Cooldowns** — Removes delays from supported weapons and usable combat equipment.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Increases damage dealt to eligible monsters during combat encounters.

### 📌 Exploration

- **Instant Tool Use** — Speeds up supported digging and interaction actions while searching for buried loot.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and load into a raid before activating movement options. Enable Fast Movement for quicker travel through the graveyard, and disable it before navigating tight areas or interacting with objects if movement becomes difficult to control.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect treasure normally during a raid and return to the flying ship to spend it on tools and gear. Unlimited Treasure can be enabled before purchasing upgrades, but it should be disabled before joining a competitive or public multiplayer session.

### World & Environment

The main loop takes players from the cursed flying ship into haunted graveyards to dig for loot, avoid hazards, and survive hostile creatures. Use Silent Movement to reduce attention from sound-sensitive monsters, and use Instant Tool Use to speed up supported digging interactions.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable God Mode before entering areas with dense enemy activity. No Cooldowns and One-Hit Defeat can simplify fights against regular monsters and supported encounters, but trainer effects may not apply to every boss or weapon in every game build.
